Cobe Manual

Everything Cobe can do, one screen at a time. Use the arrows on your remote to move and the center button (OK) to choose. Back always takes you one step back.

1. Add your sources

Go to Settings > Library and choose Add. Pick a tab:

  • Your own media: Shared Folder (a folder on your NAS), Jellyfin, Emby or Plex. These cards are yellow.
  • Your own playlists: M3U or Xtream. These cards are blue.

Fill in the fields and choose Save. For Xtream, Cobe checks your login first. If it says "No connection" or "Login refused", nothing is saved.

Each source becomes a card. The dot shows how it is doing:

  • Green: it works.
  • Amber ring: the login works, but no channels came in this time. Your old list keeps playing.
  • Red: something is wrong. The reason is written on the card.
  • Orange square: you stopped it.

Open a card to update, stop, edit or remove it, or to see what is in it. Back closes the card. Update All reloads every source, one after the other.

2. Merge everything into one

  • Merge / Sort Playlists: channels, movies and series with the same name from different playlists show up once, sorted. Off: every playlist stays on its own, in its own order.
  • Merge With Local Media: your own movies and series appear next to the ones in your playlists, in one library.

Both are switches at the top of Settings > Library, also with one source. Merge With Local Media only works while Merge / Sort Playlists is on. Merging happens by itself every time Cobe loads; your playlists are never changed.

The total at the top counts each title once when merging is on, and adds up the cards when it is off.

3. Live TV

  • Merge / Sort: this box sits in the left column, above the groups, and is ticked at the start. Cobe then shows all your playlists as one list, sorted by continent, country and type. Untick it to see each playlist on its own, in the playlist's own order. (The box shows while Merge / Sort Playlists is on in Settings > Library.)
  • Move to a group and press OK to open it.
  • Favorites: hold OK on a channel and choose Add to Favorites to find it fast. While watching, the star on the bar does the same.
  • TV Guide: see what's on now and later.
  • The right side shows a live preview of the selected channel.
  • While watching, press OK or up to show the bar, and down to show the channel list.
  • If a stream stops, Cobe automatically tries another copy of the same channel.

4. Link channels

Have the same channel in more than one playlist under different names? Hold OK on a channel and choose Link to…, so they count as one channel and back each other up. All links are listed in Settings > Linked Channels; use the bin to remove one. With Merge / Sort Playlists off, links only join channels inside the same playlist.

5. Multiview

Watch up to 4 channels at the same time. Hold OK on a channel and choose Multiview, or press the four-squares button on the player bar while watching live TV. Use the small + to add screen 3 and 4.

6. Movies and Series

Browse by genre on the left, or search. Inside each genre, films and series are also sorted into country and language folders. Open a poster to see the story, rating and the copies you can play. For series, pick a season and an episode.

A spinning wheel means Cobe is still building the list or loading a poster.

7. The player

  • For movies and series: left and right jump 10 seconds, and the center button pauses.
  • The small (i) next to LIVE, MOVIE or EPISODE shows what is on, or the story.
  • Choose audio and subtitle languages from the bar; they are listed by language name.
  • The moon button on the bar sets a sleep timer, so Cobe stops by itself.

8. Profiles and parental control

  • Give everyone in the house their own profile, with their own favorites, Continue watching and subtitles. Add one in Settings > Profiles. The first profile is the main one.
  • With 2 or more profiles, Cobe asks Who's watching? when it starts.
  • Turn on Kids for a profile to hide adult content. In a Kids profile, Settings only shows Categories, Home Screen, Subtitles and About.
  • Adult content is hidden by default. To show it, go to Settings > Adults PIN and turn on Show Adult Content.
  • Set an Adults PIN to protect it. One PIN works for all profiles. Then choose what it locks: Lock Adult Content and Lock Settings (both off at the start). Once you open locked Settings, they stay open until Cobe has been away for 30 minutes.
  • Picking a profile never asks for the PIN. Only leaving a Kids profile does.
  • Forgot your PIN? Choose Forgot PIN? under the number pad. After you sign in with your Apple Account, the PIN is removed.

9. Make it yours

  • Settings > Subtitles: size and height of subtitles, with a live preview. Each profile has its own.
  • Settings > Picture and Sound: picture and sound options.
  • Settings > Language: pick one of 20 languages.
  • Reminders: in the TV Guide, hold OK on a programme and choose Remind Me.
  • Top Shelf: put Cobe in the top row of your Apple TV home screen to see Top Movies there.

10. Subscription

Settings > Subscription shows your plan and how many days of your free week are left. One purchase works on every Apple TV with the same Apple Account.
